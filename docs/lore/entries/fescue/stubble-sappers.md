Hard-grit goblins and cinder-gnoblins who tunnel beneath the Shorn and around the Shed's foundations. They lean toward *Cut it all*—not out of piety for the Mower, like the Order of the Even Cut, but because short grass drops the best iron rust, flint chips, and Black Dew drips.

## What gnomes say

- **Rumbling:** The Sappers grew up during the Quiet Years, when the Even Cut quietly took the Shorn and hired tunnel-goblins to dig listening-galleries under the Verge without the Crown noticing.
- They trade sharp flint, rusted Shed-nails, and scrap tin to anyone who pays in pebbles or tallow, and their Grub-Master **Nibs Rustcap** sells information to both **High Edger Sedgewick** and **Knight-Commander Rowan Ironsod**.

## Sad note

Most Stubble-Sappers are goblins whose moss-beds were scoured bare during the Backflow; they now live in dry chalk-tunnels where nothing green grows and their joints creak like dry bark.
