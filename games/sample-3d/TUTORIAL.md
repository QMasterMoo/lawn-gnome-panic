# Lawn Gnome Panic: How to Level Up

Your gnome gets stronger in two ways. Inside a run you level up and pick upgrades. Between runs you spend acorns on permanent upgrades. This guide covers both.

## The short version

1. Kill mowers. They drop green XP gems.
2. Pick up gems and acorns. Both give XP.
3. Fill the XP bar. A **LEVEL UP!** screen appears with three cards.
4. Pick a card. Weapons and passives make your run stronger.
5. When the run ends, spend the acorns you collected on permanent upgrades in the lobby.

## Step 1: Get XP

There are two sources of XP during a run.

**Mowers drop XP gems.** Every mower you destroy drops green XP gems worth:

| Mower | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Push Reel Mower (the slow one) | 1 |
| Green Mower | 2 |
| Yellow Mower | 4 |
| Red Mower (the fast one) | 7 |
| THE COMBINE (boss) | 100 |

**Acorns give XP too.** Each acorn you pick up is worth 3 XP. Acorns also count toward your permanent upgrades, so they are worth picking up twice.

Gems and acorns fly toward you when they are close. Walk over them to collect them.

**Night is worth more.** While it is night, all XP you earn is multiplied by 1.25.

**Wisdom makes everything faster.** The permanent upgrade *Ancient Gnome Wisdom* adds +10% XP per level, up to five levels.

## Step 2: Level up

Each level needs more XP than the last. Here is how much XP each level costs:

| Level | XP to reach the next level | Total XP so far |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 → 2 | 9 | 9 |
| 2 → 3 | 16 | 25 |
| 3 → 4 | 26 | 51 |
| 4 → 5 | 37 | 88 |
| 5 → 6 | 50 | 138 |
| 6 → 7 | 64 | 202 |
| 7 → 8 | 79 | 281 |
| 8 → 9 | 96 | 377 |
| 9 → 10 | 113 | 490 |

When you reach the XP needed, the game slows down for about a second to celebrate. Then the **LEVEL UP!** screen appears. If you earn enough XP for more than one level at once, you get one screen for each level, in order.

Levelling up does not heal you, but there is one card that does (see below).

## Step 3: Pick a card

Each level-up shows three cards. Choose one by:

- Pressing **1**, **2** or **3** (the number keys, or the numpad)
- Clicking or tapping the card

**Card types:**

- **Weapons (gold border).** Weapons fire on their own. A card marked **NEW!** gives you a weapon you do not have yet. A card marked **LV 2**, **LV 3** and so on upgrades a weapon you already own. Each weapon has a maximum level.
- **Passives (blue border).** Passives boost your gnome, for example more damage, a faster cooldown, more hearts or a bigger pickup range. Each passive has a maximum level.
- **Mulch Sandwich (pink border).** Restores 1 heart. This card appears only when every weapon and passive is already at its maximum level, so you will not see it early.

**Rerolls.** Press **R** or click **REROLL** to swap all three cards for new ones. Each reroll uses one of your rerolls. You start a run with none, so you need the *Lucky Mushroom* upgrade first. Rerolls are per run, not per level.

**Tip:** Choose upgrades that fit your character's style. You cannot take everything, so pick a few weapons and passives and make them strong.

## Step 4: Survive the run

A run ends when you lose all your hearts. You start with 3 hearts, and you can get more through upgrades.

Keep your XP flowing:

- **Stay near mowers.** The more you fight, the more XP you get.
- **Pick up the acorns mowers drop.** They are worth XP and acorns.
- **Use the Pocket Magnet upgrade.** It pulls XP and acorns toward you from further away.
- **Watch for items.** Some rare world items, like the compost bin, give a free level-up when you pick them up.

## Step 5: Spend acorns in the lobby

Acorns you collect during a run are saved to your account. You spend them in the lobby on **PRIMITIVE POWERS**, which are permanent upgrades. Each upgrade has several levels, and each level costs more acorns than the last.

| Upgrade | Effect per level | Max level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cardio | +0.5s sprint | 5 |
| Second Wind | +0.3 stamina regen/s, shorter delay | 5 |
| Springy Boots | +0.4 jump power | 5 |
| Stubby Legs++ | +0.3 move speed | 5 |
| Extra Beard | +1 max heart | 3 |
| Pocket Magnet | +0.75m pickup range | 4 |
| Ancient Gnome Wisdom | +10% XP | 5 |
| Lucky Mushroom | +1 reroll per run | 3 |

Extra Beard is the most expensive to upgrade, but it gives you a permanent extra heart for every run.

The lobby also shows your stats. Check it to see how your upgrades add up.

## Solo levels

In **SOLO** mode you can pick a level from the **LV** buttons in the lobby. Each level is its own run, and you win it by beating its boss fight. Beating a level unlocks the next one.

- **Level 1:** The Front Lawn, with one combine.
- **Level 2:** Mow Harder, with two combines. The mowers are tougher and faster.
- **Level 3:** Mow Hardest With a Vengeance, with three combines.

When a run ends, all the acorns you collected are added to your account, win or lose. You also get bonus acorns: 1 for every 10 seconds you survive, and 50 for each combine you defeat.

## Quick reference

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move | WASD or arrow keys |
| Jump (press again in the air to double jump) | Space |
| Sprint | Shift (uses stamina) |
| Look around | Mouse after clicking the game, or Q and E |
| Pick a level-up card | 1, 2, 3 or click |
| Reroll | R |
| Pause | Esc |

Gamepad and touch controls work too.

## Tips

- Every run is random, so do not plan too far ahead.
- Permanent upgrades matter most early on. Buy Extra Beard and Ancient Gnome Wisdom first if you want to go further.
- Dying is part of the game. Your acorns and permanent upgrades survive, so the next run starts stronger.
